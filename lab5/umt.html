About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It was originally established as the Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science under Universiti Pertanian Malaysia in 1979.

In 1996, the centre became a branch campus known as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). Later, it was renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001.

On 1 February 2007, the institution was upgraded to a full public university and renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu. Today, UMT offers various academic programmes and focuses on marine science, fisheries, technology and environmental studies.
